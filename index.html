Financial RAG — Retrieval-Augmented QA over SEC Filings

GenAI · RAG · Retrieval Evaluation · 2026

Retrieval-augmented QA over ~30 large-cap 10-K filings, built around retrieval engineering and evaluation rather than a thin wrapper on a vector store. BM25 and FAISS dense search are fused with Reciprocal Rank Fusion, then reranked with BGE, with abstention gating and citation verification; served from FastAPI with Qwen2.5-7B and tracked in MLflow.

  • Every component was chosen by benchmark: e5-base-v2 won a four-model embedding bake-off and reranking added 13% to Recall@5. Evaluated on a 180-question held-out set with deterministic IR metrics and independent citation-grounding checks: Recall@5 of 0.650, faithfulness of 0.808 and a 7.5% hallucination rate.
  • Retrieved documents are treated as untrusted and tested against a seven-attack prompt-injection suite, four of which were stopped. A query-rewriting step was benchmarked and then shipped disabled after it measurably hurt retrieval; the shortfalls are reported, not hidden.

Production ML Deployment — Steel Surface Defect Classification

MLOps · FastAPI · Terraform · 2026

The full MLOps path for a 6-class steel surface defect classifier: FastAPI serving, an MLflow model registry with alias-based promotion behind validation gates, and infrastructure defined in Terraform on AWS ECS/Fargate, backed by 34 tests.

  • PSI and confidence drift signals feed automated retraining that leaves production untouched when a candidate model fails validation.
  • ResNet-18 was chosen over EfficientNet-B0 on measured CPU latency (17.1 vs 24.5 ms p95 at equal accuracy). Load testing surfaced a synchronous-inference bottleneck; clearing it raised throughput from 134.7 to 193.4 requests/second and cut p99 latency from 4,800 ms to 810 ms.

Multimodal Wildfire Detection — M.S. Capstone

ASU Capstone · Computer Vision · NLP · 2026

Fuses a ResNet vision model, a fine-tuned DistilBERT text model and geospatial signals (spaCy NER, haversine distance, temporal decay) across 75,709 NASA FIRMS detections clustered into 324 events.

  • An ablation study showed that fusion lifted ROC-AUC from 0.556 to 0.789 in conditions where imagery alone was unreliable.
  • Two data-leakage bugs were found and fixed before any result was trusted: near-duplicate tiles split across train and test, and template leakage that had inflated text accuracy to 100%, corrected to an honest 85.3%. The pipeline is covered by 116 tests, with MLflow tracking and a FastAPI serving layer.
PyTorch ResNet DistilBERT spaCy NER

Network Intrusion Detection with Machine Learning — Research Paper

Machine Learning · Cybersecurity · ASU · 2024

A co-authored research paper benchmarking seven classifiers — Decision Tree, Random Forest, KNN, SVC, Gradient Boosting, AdaBoost and XGBoost — across NSL-KDD and CIC-IDS2017 (roughly 690k records, 41 features reduced to the 30 most predictive via ExtraTrees), on a single 80/20 seed split so the binary and multi-class runs stayed directly comparable.

  • The framing classifies attack type rather than the usual normal-vs-attack split, so a response can be matched to the intrusion.
  • Random Forest transferred best at 99.77% binary and 99.71% multi-class, while AdaBoost collapsed from 78% to 62%. A macro-F1 of 0.69–0.94 exposed weak recall on rare attack classes such as Heartbleed (0.50), hidden entirely behind the headline accuracy figure.
